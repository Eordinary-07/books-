## ✅ Free & legal substitutes for this subject

- ***Lessons In Electric Circuits* Vol 3 — Semiconductors (IN THIS REPO ✅)** — 535 pages: diodes, rectifiers, bipolar transistors, JFETs/MOSFETs, amplifiers, op-amps, active filters, oscillators, and power circuits — all explained from first principles with worked examples.
  `00-Common-Library/Lessons-in-Electric-Circuits/LEC-Vol3-Semiconductors.pdf`
- **Texas Instruments — *Op Amps for Everyone*** — Free, complete, authoritative PDF from TI. Covers ideal op-amp theory through real-world design and filter synthesis.
  <https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/slod006b/slod006b.pdf>
- **Analog Devices — *Linear Circuit Design Handbook* / *Op Amp Applications Handbook*** — Free full engineering handbooks from ADI. Superb on feedback, filters, noise and instrumentation.
  <https://www.analog.com/en/education/education-library/linear-circuit-design-handbook.html>
- **MIT OCW 6.012 — Microelectronic Devices and Circuits** — Free MIT course covering semiconductor physics, MOS/BJT devices and amplifiers at a depth above GATE needs.
  <https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-012-microelectronic-devices-and-circuits-spring-2009/>
- **NPTEL — Analog Electronic Circuits / Analog ICs** — Indian-syllabus-aligned; good match for the GATE EE analog section.
  <https://archive.nptel.ac.in/courses/108/>

## 📅 Suggested study order

1. One theory book only — do **not** read two in parallel.
2. Free NPTEL/MIT lectures for anything that won't click.
3. Practice book for numericals.
4. PYQs, timed. This is where the marks actually come from.
