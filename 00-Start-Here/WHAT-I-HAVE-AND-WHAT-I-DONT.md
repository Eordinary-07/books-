**Why so few?** Because almost everything on your list is a current Indian-market commercial title (Khanna, Dhanpat Rai, Nodia, PHI). Publishers don't license those for free lending. That's the whole story.

**Try NDLI for the Indian titles** — [ndl.iitkgp.ac.in](https://ndl.iitkgp.ac.in), free for every Indian student, built by IIT Kharagpur. It's the best shot for Bimbhra, Sawhney, Kanodia, Rajput, J.B. Gupta. I could not query it from this sandbox, so I'm telling you to check rather than claiming they're there.

### One special case — Oppenheim's *Signals and Systems*
On Internet Archive it is **"Preview Only"** — you can search it but not read it. So despite being "on the Internet Archive," **you cannot study from it.** Use **MIT OCW 6.003** (taught by Oppenheim himself, free) plus **Think DSP** from this repo instead. This is exactly the trap to avoid: a book appearing on IA doesn't mean it's readable.

2. **Borrow Kothari & Nagrath** — that's your Power Systems main text, free, ₹600–700 saved.
